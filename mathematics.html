Research Papers

Galois Theory Through Cyclotomic Extensions - This is a research paper I wrote on Galois theory through the Directed Reading Program (DRP) in Mathematics at UC Berkeley in Spring 2026, under the supervision and mentorship of Sai Sanjeev Balakrishnan. The paper explores the fundamental concepts of Galois theory, specifically focusing on cyclotomic extensions. It would be helpful for readers to have a background in abstract algebra, particularly in field theory and group theory, to fully appreciate the content of the paper, but little prior knowledge is required.
